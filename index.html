How to colour

  1. Choose a picture in Explore.
  2. Tap a numbered colour at the bottom of the screen. Every area with that number is highlighted.
  3. Tap the highlighted areas to fill them in.
  4. Pinch to zoom in on small areas, and drag to move around the picture.
  5. Stuck? Use Hint to find the next area, Fill next to colour one for you, or Undo to take back a tap.

Your progress saves automatically. Finished and unfinished pictures are in My Art.

Free and premium pictures

Five pictures are free. A single one-time purchase unlocks 99 more across four collections, from Easy to Intricate. There is no subscription and there are no adverts.

Purchases are in For grown-ups (the person icon at the top of Explore). A short maths question keeps this area for adults.

Restoring your purchase

If you've bought premium pictures before, on this or another iPhone or iPad:

  1. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.
  2. Open For grown-ups, answer the maths question and tap Restore purchases.

Restoring is always free. You'll never be charged twice for the same purchase.

Troubleshooting

My progress isn't on my other device. Progress is stored only on the device you colour on. It doesn't sync between devices, but it is included in your normal iCloud or computer backup of that device.

I deleted the app and lost my pictures. Deleting the app deletes its saved progress. Your premium purchase isn't lost: reinstall and use Restore purchases.

The purchase button says "Contacting the App Store…". Check your internet connection, then close and reopen For grown-ups. If you use Screen Time, check that in-app purchases are allowed.

A child asked to buy and nothing happened. With Ask to Buy, the purchase waits for approval from the family organiser. The pictures unlock once it's approved.

I'd like a refund. Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and choose the purchase.

I want to start again. To clear one picture, open it, tap the ••• menu and choose Restart picture. To clear everything, open For grown-ups and choose Clear all progress and favourites.
