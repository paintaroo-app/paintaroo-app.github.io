Privacy policy

Paintaroo: Colour by Number · Last updated 8 October 2026

The short version

Paintaroo does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you colour stays on your device.

Who we are

Paintaroo is made by Kash Ali, an independent developer based in the United Kingdom. For any privacy question, email paintaroo.cbn@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

The app saves the following only on your iPhone or iPad, so you can carry on where you left off:

  • which areas of each picture you've coloured, and your undo history
  • your favourite pictures
  • your settings, such as haptics and larger numbers

This information is never sent to us or anyone else. It may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup of the device, which Apple's privacy policy covers.

Purchases

Premium pictures are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app only checks with Apple whether the premium pictures have been bought on your Apple Account. Apple gives developers anonymous sales totals, which don't identify you. See Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing pictures

When a picture is finished, a grown-up can choose to share it. The app opens the standard iOS share sheet, and you decide where the picture goes. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.

Children

Paintaroo is designed to be safe for the whole family. We don't collect information from anyone, including children. Purchases, sharing and clearing saved artwork are behind a grown-ups' check (a maths question).

If you email us

If you contact us, we'll use your email address and message only to reply to you. We don't add you to any mailing list, and we delete support emails once your question is resolved, normally within 12 months.

Deleting your data

To delete everything the app has saved, open For grown-ups and choose Clear all progress and favourites, or delete the app. To delete a support email, just ask us.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what information we hold about you and ask us to delete it. Because the app collects nothing, the only information we could ever hold is an email you've sent us. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
